About Sauna Buddies

Who we are, why we meet, and where the group is headed.

Our story so far

Sauna Buddies is a group of members who came together and decided their gatherings needed more than good company — they needed a shared direction.

Guiding philosophy

“Unity does not mean sameness; it means oneness of purpose.”

“Oneness is the secret of all things.”

These two ideas anchor how the group approaches disagreement, diversity of opinion, and decision-making: differences of background or view are expected, but everyone is working toward the same purpose.

How the group works

Shared leadership

Members take turns leading discussions or workshops on topics such as career and financial growth, leadership, and relationships and family — an idea proposed by Wilson.

Welfare

Financial welfare was raised as a discussion point, with a task force working through what a fair contribution structure could look like.

Structure & governance

The group is working toward a constitution, formal registration, and a bank account, alongside settling on a name and identity.

Where things stand

Sauna Buddies is early in its journey. A task force — Patrick, Dr. Nyongesa, Bishop, Chief and Wilson — is working through the group name, constitution, registration and a proposed monthly contribution. The leadership team has been given the mandate to bring a group name to members. Full details are recorded meeting by meeting in the meeting archive.

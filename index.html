Our purpose

Sauna Buddies began as a gathering of members who wanted more than casual meetups — a group with a shared direction, built around welfare, mutual growth and honest fellowship. The group is still shaping its identity: its name, its constitution and its structures are being built deliberately, one meeting at a time, rather than rushed.

What holds the group together isn't uniformity of background or opinion. It's a shared sense of purpose, and a commitment to figure things out together.

  • Welfare first. Members raised the need for a financial welfare structure to support one another.
  • Shared leadership. Members take turns leading discussions on topics like career, finances, leadership and family.
  • Built to last. A constitution, registration and a bank account are underway so the group can grow responsibly.
